## Why it matters

Interfaces with dozens of inputs are where forgotten bindings and dead knobs hide. Related values are easier to keep consistent when grouped.

## How to fix

Group related inputs into one JSON input (`fromJSON(inputs.test-config)`), split the workflow, or move constants into the callee.
